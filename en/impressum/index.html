Reservation rules

Reservations are limited to the next three days. The exact counting method is still being agreed for this draft.

This preview uses sample values: 1–8 guests, half-hour time steps, a 90-minute stay and a 15-minute buffer. Actual operating rules are not yet approved. Space must be available for the whole visit. Suggested times do not hold seats.

This preview does not save real bookings or send messages. In the future service, booking and email delivery will have separate states: a missing email does not by itself mean a booking failed. If the result is unclear, do not book again. Change and cancellation terms will be added once approved.

What brings us together

Pendi is imagined as a place for evenings shared: a first conversation, familiar faces and a reason to stay a little longer. This draft puts people and a relaxed, attentive atmosphere at the centre. The team will help shape and approve the final brand story.
