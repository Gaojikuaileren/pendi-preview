Privacy

1. Controller The controller for personal data processed on this website is the operator named in the legal notice. For privacy questions, please use the email address given there. 2. Overview We only process data needed to run this website and to handle your reservation. We use no analytics or tracking services, no advertising or social media plugins and no externally loaded fonts. Simply visiting the website sets no cookies; the only exception is a strictly necessary session cookie when you use the reservation form (section 5). 3. Hosting and server log files The website is hosted by STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. When you open the website, the server automatically processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested page, amount of data transferred, referring page, browser and operating system. This serves to deliver and secure the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). STRATO stores IP addresses for no more than seven days and anonymises them afterwards. 4. Abuse prevention To protect the website and the reservation form against automated attacks, we store a pseudonymised identifier derived from the IP address (a cryptographic hash) and request counts for short time windows (usually 10 to 30 minutes). Entries are deleted automatically during normal operation once the window has passed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). 5. Online reservations When you reserve online, we process your name, email address, date and time, party size and chosen language to carry out your reservation, including confirmation, changes and cancellation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Online reservations are not possible without this information; you can also reach us by phone. The form sets a strictly necessary session cookie that is deleted when you close the browser. In addition, the browser keeps a random identifier in its session storage for the duration of the session so that a request is not submitted twice (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Resend is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). A data processing agreement is in place with Resend. 7. Contact and job applications If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.
